How the BusBuddy Heuristic & Prediction Engine Works

Instead of an opaque black box, our university prototype implements a transparent, multi-factor weighting model. The system predicts arrival delay and crowd density by evaluating four primary features:

Feature 1
Time-of-Day Curve

Non-linear crowd surges during 08:00–10:00 AM (multiplier: 1.45x) and 04:00–06:00 PM.

Feature 2
Spatial Bottleneck

Kharar Highway Flyover road repairs add +4 mins delay index; Landran bypass flows smoothly.

Feature 3
Crowd Consensus

Recent passenger crowd reports adjust the baseline occupancy by ±8% based on consensus.

Feature 4
Historical Variance

Stop dwell times based on 30-day recorded CTU corridor punctuality statistics.

Predicted_ETA = Scheduled_ETA + (Bottleneck_Factor * W_traffic) + (Peak_Multiplier * W_rush) + Report_Variance

Future Machine Learning Pipeline & Deployment Roadmap

Future Architecture

For future deployment, our modular data layer is architected to be coupled with an end-to-end Machine Learning pipeline:

1. IoT Edge Sensing

Overhead infrared door-break sensors & ESP32 cameras running lightweight MobileNet to count boarding/alighting counts.

2. Spatio-Temporal GCN

A Spatio-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network (ST-GCN) to model bus stop graph topology and traffic propagation.

3. Gradient Boosted Delay Engine

XGBoost / LightGBM regressor predicting transit delay intervals with real-time confidence intervals.
